# 6. The Major Anchor
id: ch6
targetScaleType: major
focus: Harmonic Stability
mission: Learn the "Anchor" notes of the Major Pentatonic—the 2nd and 5th.
description: While the 3rd and 6th give the Major Pentatonic its color, the 2nd and 5th provide the solid foundation and stability for your melodies.

## Tutorial Step: The Major 2nd
instruction: The Major 2nd (2) adds a suspended, airy quality. It is located 2 frets above any Root on the same string.
targetInterval: 2
actionText: Find the Major 2nd (2)

## Tutorial Step: The Perfect 5th (Major)
instruction: The 5th (5) is the most stable interval. In Major, it is often found one string higher and two frets up from the root.
targetInterval: 5
actionText: Locate the Perfect 5th (5)
