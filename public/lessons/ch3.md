# 3. The Full Frame
id: ch3
targetScaleType: minor
focus: Full Scale Awareness
mission: Identify the remaining intervals—the Perfect 4th and Minor 7th.
description: Complete your understanding of the Minor Pentatonic by mastering the 4th and b7. These notes provide the flow and tension needed for sophisticated soloing.

## Tutorial Step: The Perfect 4th
instruction: The 4th (4) is a stable, melodic note. In this box, you'll find it directly 'below' (one string higher) its root on the same fret. 
targetInterval: 4
actionText: Find the Perfect 4th (4)

## Tutorial Step: The Minor 7th
instruction: The Minor 7th (b7) is the 'blues' tension note. It is always 2 frets below any Root on the same string.
targetInterval: b7
actionText: Locate the Minor 7th (b7)
