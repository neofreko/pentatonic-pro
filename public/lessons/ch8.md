# 8. Transposing to Any Key
id: ch8
targetScaleType: minor
focus: Fretboard Navigation
mission: Learn how to move the Minor Pentatonic box to any key by finding the Root on the 6th string.
description: The magic of the guitar is that patterns are movable. Once you know "Box 1", you can play in any key just by shifting your starting position.

## Tutorial Step: The Low E Anchor
instruction: To play in any minor key, you must first find its Root note on the Low E (6th) string. For example, to play in A Minor, find the A note on the 5th fret.
targetInterval: R
actionText: Locate the Root on the 6th string

## Tutorial Step: Moving to G Minor
instruction: Now, imagine you need to play in G Minor. Use the Key selector at the top of the app to change the key to G. Notice how the whole pattern shifts to start on the 3rd fret!
targetInterval: R
actionText: Click the Root in G Minor (3rd fret)

## Tutorial Step: Moving to C Minor
instruction: Finally, change the key to C. The Root is now on the 8th fret of the Low E string. The physical shape of the box remains exactly the same.
targetInterval: R
actionText: Find the Root in C Minor (8th fret)

## Proficiency Challenge
type: FIND_INTERVALS
targetInterval: R
description: Find all Root (R) notes in your current key. Try changing the key and repeating this challenge to master transposition!
requiredCount: 3
