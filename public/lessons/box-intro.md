# 2. Understanding the "Box"
id: box-intro
targetScaleType: minor
focus: Hand Positioning
mission: Learn why we call it a "Box" and how to position your hand to play it.
description: A "Box" is a 4-fret area where you can play a whole scale without moving your hand. It's the secret to fast and easy guitar playing.

## Tutorial Step: The Index Finger Anchor
instruction: Place your hand so your index finger is on the 5th fret. This is your "anchor". Every note in this box will be reached by one of your four fingers without sliding your hand.
targetInterval: R
actionText: Click the Root (R) on the 5th fret

## Tutorial Step: The Box Width
instruction: Notice how all the notes in this position fall within a 4-fret span (frets 5 to 8). This "rectangular" area on the fretboard is what we call the "Box".
targetInterval: b3
actionText: Click the b3 (8th fret)

## Tutorial Step: Staying Inside the Box
instruction: Explore the notes on the next string. Stay within the same fret range. This vertical movement across strings while staying in the same fret "lane" creates the Box shape.
targetInterval: 4
actionText: Click the 4th interval

type: FIND_INTERVALS
