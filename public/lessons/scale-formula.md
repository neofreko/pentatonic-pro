# 2. The Scale Formula (W/H)
id: scale-formula
targetScaleType: chromatic
focus: Scale Construction
mission: Learn how Scales are built using a specific sequence of Whole (W) and Half (H) steps.
description: Every scale has a "recipe" or formula. Instead of memorizing random dots, you can build any scale if you know its sequence of steps.

## Tutorial Step: The "WWH" Concept
instruction: Most Western scales use a combination of Whole Steps (W) and Half Steps (H). For example, the **Major Scale** formula is: **W - W - H - W - W - W - H**. Let's practice building a sequence. Start by finding your Root.
targetInterval: R
actionText: Click the Root (R)

## Tutorial Step: The Whole Step (W)
instruction: A Whole Step (W) is 2 frets. Move up 2 frets from the Root. This lands you on the 2nd degree. This is the first "W" in the Major formula.
targetInterval: 2
actionText: Move up a Whole Step (2)

## Tutorial Step: The Pentatonic Recipe
instruction: The **Minor Pentatonic** has a different recipe: **(W+H) - W - W - (W+H) - W**. The first step is a "Minor 3rd" jump (3 frets). Move 3 frets up from your Root.
targetInterval: b3
actionText: Jump 3 frets (b3)

## Proficiency Challenge
type: FIND_INTERVALS
targetInterval: b3
description: Find all the notes that are exactly a "Minor 3rd" jump (3 frets) away from a Root note. These are the first steps of the Minor Pentatonic recipe.
