# 1. Fretboard Geometry
id: ch1
targetScaleType: chromatic
focus: Distance & Movement
mission: Understand the building blocks of scales—Half Steps and Whole Steps.
description: Before playing scales, you must understand how to measure distance on the strings. Every scale is just a sequence of "Small" and "Large" steps.

## Tutorial Step: The Half Step (H)
instruction: A Half Step is the smallest distance on the guitar. It is exactly 1 fret away. Move from your Root **Up** (towards the guitar body / higher fret numbers) by 1 fret. This interval is called a Minor 2nd (b2).
targetInterval: b2
actionText: Move up 1 fret (Higher Pitch)

## Tutorial Step: The Whole Step (W)
instruction: A Whole Step is exactly 2 frets away. It skips one fret in between. Move from your Root **Up** 2 frets (Higher Pitch). This interval is called a Major 2nd (2).
targetInterval: 2
actionText: Move up 2 frets (Higher Pitch)
